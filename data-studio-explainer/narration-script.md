## Takeaways for accounting teams, one per step

- **Connect:** data starts flowing sooner after contract, with less back-and-forth with IT.
- **Map:** accounts and entities line up with your checklist, reconciliations and reports.
- **Test:** errors show up before go-live, not halfway through a reconciliation.
- **Publish:** every module reads the same numbers, and every version is kept for audit.
- **Monitor:** you hear about a data problem before it reaches your close, in words you can act on.

The 0 hours, under 60 minutes and 90%+ figures are product targets from the PRDs, not measured results.
The sample data is illustrative: the account numbers, amounts, company codes and run logs are made up.
